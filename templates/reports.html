Report Types

The Climate Action Orchestrator's Reporting Agent can generate various report types to meet different stakeholder needs and compliance requirements.

Description: Standard emissions inventory report following the Greenhouse Gas Protocol framework.

Contents:

  • Organizational and operational boundaries
  • Emissions by scope (Scope 1, 2, and 3)
  • Emissions by gas (CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, etc.)
  • Emissions by source category
  • Base year emissions and recalculations
  • Calculation methodologies and emissions factors

Use Cases: Corporate sustainability reporting, environmental management systems, regulatory compliance

Description: Climate-related financial disclosure following the Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) framework.

Contents:

  • Governance around climate-related risks and opportunities
  • Strategy for addressing climate-related risks and opportunities
  • Risk management processes for climate-related risks
  • Metrics and targets used to assess and manage climate-related risks and opportunities

Use Cases: Investor communications, annual sustainability reports, ESG disclosures

Description: Responses to the CDP (formerly Carbon Disclosure Project) Climate Change Questionnaire.

Contents:

  • Governance and business strategy questions
  • Risks and opportunities assessment
  • Emissions data and calculation methodologies
  • Emissions reduction initiatives and targets
  • Carbon pricing mechanisms
  • Verification and assurance

Use Cases: CDP submission, stakeholder communication, ESG ratings improvement

Description: High-level summary of climate performance for executive leadership.

Contents:

  • Key emissions metrics and trends
  • Progress against reduction targets
  • Top sustainability opportunities
  • Benchmarking against industry peers
  • Financial implications and business case
  • Recommended next steps

Use Cases: Executive briefings, board presentations, strategic planning
